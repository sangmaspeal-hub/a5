Draping Engineering

The Kinetic Grace of the 45-Degree Bias Cut

Pioneered in the 1930s and refined in our New York atelier, true bias draping turns rigid woven fabric into a living, responsive second skin. Unlike straight-grain garments that hang rigidly, bias-cut silk drapes liquidly along the diagonal.

This allows the garment to skim curves effortlessly without gripping or pulling, delivering unmatched freedom of movement whether walking a gallery hall or stepping into a gala.

  • ✓ Zero synthetic spandex or elastane microplastics
  • ✓ Hand-suspended hanging for 72 hours before hem calibration
  • ✓ French seam finishing with 18 stitches per inch

The Spectrum of Living Ruby Pigments

Rubia Tinctorum (Madder Root)

Sappanwood & Red Sandalwood

Harvested from sustainably managed heartwood plantations, brazilin extracts create luminous crimson tones with gentle optical light refraction.

Algal Carotenoids

Zero-footprint marine microalgae bio-fermentations provide deep, lightfast scarlet dyes that bond permanently to protein fibers without heavy metal mordants.

Client Advisory

Frequently Asked Questions

Essential guidance on bespoke orders, sizing, care, and salon appointments.

Our bespoke process involves three distinct appointments: an initial styling and 36-point body measurement consultation, a muslin toile fitting to verify structural drape, and a final fitting in your chosen noble fabric. Production typically spans 4 to 6 weeks from initial consultation to delivery.

We work exclusively with 100% natural, biodegradable noble fibers, including Grade 6A Italian Mulberry silk (19 to 30 momme), double-faced 17.5-micron Australian merino wool, Grade A Mongolian cashmere, and certified organic linen. We never incorporate synthetic polyester or elastane.

Yes. We ship worldwide via carbon-neutral secure express couriers with comprehensive transit insurance. For international clients seeking multi-garment capsule wardrobes, our master tailors host private trunk salon fittings in London, Paris, Tokyo, and Zurich biannually.

Silk garments should be professionally eco-cleaned by specialist green dry cleaners or gently hand-washed in cool water with pH-neutral silk wash. Wool and cashmere coats should be aired between wears on broad cedar hangers and spot-cleaned as needed. We provide lifetime garment restoration and minor alteration services.
