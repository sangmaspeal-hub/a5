Sartorial & Material Disclaimer

1. General Information & Educational Monograph Disclaimer

The sartorial monographs, textile physics analyses, pattern drafting frameworks, historical dye chronologies, and garment care instructions published on rubyweardrift.com (including our Scholarly Couture Journal) are provided exclusively for general informational, educational, and aesthetic enrichment. While our master patternmakers and textile technologists strive to maintain absolute scientific and historical accuracy, we make no representations or warranties regarding the universal reproducibility of couture techniques outside our controlled atelier environment.

2. Natural Fiber Characteristics & Botanical Dye Nuance

Our bespoke creations are handcrafted utilizing 100% natural, unadulterated noble raw materials, including Grade 6A Mulberry silk, extra-fine Australian merino wool, and Mongolian cashmere. Natural protein fibers exhibit organic microscopic variations in slub texture, luster refraction, and fiber crimp. Furthermore, garments dyed with organic madder root, sappanwood, and algal pigments may display subtle micro-tonal variations across dye lots. These natural characteristics are celebrated hallmarks of genuine artisanal sericulture and botanical dyeing, rather than manufacturing defects.

3. Garment Care & Maintenance Advisory

Due to the delicate nature of pure Mulberry silk and unlined double-faced wool, garments must be cared for strictly according to our published preservation protocols. Silk pieces should never be subjected to chlorinated water, machine agitation, or direct harsh sunlight exposure. We strongly recommend professional cleaning by specialist green dry cleaners who utilize hydrocarbon or liquid silicone solvents. Ruby Wear Drift disclaims liability for garment shrinkage, fiber degradation, or color migration resulting from improper commercial dry cleaning or domestic washing methods.

4. Anatomical Fit & Sizing Disclosures

Sartorial fit across our ready-to-wear capsule collection is engineered according to our proprietary architectural block patterns. Because human body proportions and postural pitch vary substantially, ready-to-wear pieces may require minor localized adjustments (such as sleeve length or trouser hem break) to achieve a bespoke fit. We provide complimentary tailoring consultations at our New York salon for all capsule pieces.

5. Third-Party Links & Academic References

Our website and journal monographs may contain hyperlinks to independent textile testing institutes, historical costume archives, sericulture research bodies, or environmental certification portals. These external links are provided solely for user reference and academic validation. Ruby Wear Drift does not endorse, control, or assume liability for the accuracy, content, or privacy practices of third-party domains.

6. Testimonials & Patron Endorsements Disclaimer

Patron reviews, editorial reflections, and client testimonials displayed on this platform represent the individual experiences and subjective stylistic assessments of verified patrons. Results, aesthetic resonance, and garment wear lifespans may vary based on personal usage, frequency of wear, and maintenance compliance.

8. Bespoke Artisan Tolerances and Handcraft Disclosures

Every bespoke garment constructed at Ruby Wear Drift is individually hand-cut and hand-stitched by master artisans. Because each piece is sculpted manually rather than mass-produced by automated robotic machinery, microscopic artisan variations of up to 2 millimeters along seam lines or hand-pad stitching patterns represent authentic hallmarks of genuine haute couture craftsmanship rather than manufacturing defects.
