Privacy Policy & Data Protection Protocol

Ruby Wear Drift • Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Global Operations Standard

1. Commitment to Privacy & Data Stewardship

At Ruby Wear Drift (“we,” “our,” “us,” or the “Maison”), we hold the privacy, personal autonomy, and digital confidentiality of our patrons in the highest esteem. This Privacy Policy outlines our comprehensive practices regarding the collection, processing, safeguarding, and international transmission of personal information acquired via our official website rubyweardrift.com, bespoke tailoring concierge services, private fitting salon bookings, newsletter subscriptions, and client consultations.

Our governance framework is meticulously structured in full adherence to international data protection mandates, including the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the UK General Data Protection Regulation and Data Protection Act 2018, the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA), the Canadian PIPEDA, and the New York SHIELD Act.

2. Categories of Information Collected

In delivering bespoke haute couture tailoring, luxury capsule wardrobes, and client concierge services, we collect information across several distinct categories:

  • Personal Contact Identifiers: Full legal names, title/honorific preferences, physical shipping and billing addresses, electronic mail addresses, direct mobile telephone numbers, and unique client profile numbers.
  • Sartorial Fit & Anatomical Measurements: Precise 36-point anatomical body measurements, garment posture adjustments, fabric and color preferences, custom monogramming requests, and historical bespoke tailoring pattern files.
  • Technical and Network Telemetry: Internet Protocol (IP) addresses, browser classification, operating system details, referring/exit URLs, browsing duration, clickstream paths, and device display metrics.
  • Financial Transaction Data: Encrypted payment tokens processed through PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant gateway partners for bespoke deposits and garment purchases. We do not store raw credit card numbers on our servers.

3. Lawful Bases and Legitimate Purposes for Processing

We process personal data solely in accordance with established lawful bases under GDPR Article 6:

  • Contractual Performance: Constructing bespoke garments, managing fitting salon appointments, fulfilling global boutique shipments, and transmitting order status notifications.
  • Legitimate Business Interests: Refining our textile procurement forecasts, improving website responsiveness, protecting our digital infrastructure from fraud, and conducting non-invasive sartorial research.
  • Explicit Consent: Sending private salon invitations, seasonal capsule previews, and scholarly couture monographs where you have opted in.
  • Statutory and Regulatory Compliance: Satisfying international trade customs mandates, maintaining fiscal accounting records, and cooperating with lawful government subpoenas.

4. Non-Sale of Personal Information & Data Sharing

Ruby Wear Drift does not sell, monetize, rent, or trade personal data to third-party data brokers or marketing aggregators. We disclose data strictly to vetted service providers who operate under strict non-disclosure and data protection agreements:

  • Secure cloud infrastructure and database hosting partners.
  • PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment processing networks.
  • Carbon-neutral international courier and logistics providers.
  • Legal, accounting, and regulatory compliance advisors.

5. California Consumer Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA Notice)

California residents maintain specific statutory rights regarding their personal information:

  • Right to Know: Request disclosure of the specific categories and pieces of personal information collected over the preceding 12 months.
  • Right to Delete: Request the deletion of personal information, subject to statutory record-keeping exceptions.
  • Right to Correct: Request the prompt rectification of inaccurate or outdated personal records.
  • Right to Opt-Out: Exercise the right to opt-out of the sharing or processing of personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: Receive equal service quality and pricing regardless of exercising statutory privacy rights.

6. European Economic Area (EEA) and UK Resident Rights (GDPR)

Visitors and patrons from the EEA and United Kingdom hold comprehensive statutory rights under GDPR Articles 15 through 22, including the right of data access, rectification, erasure (the “Right to be Forgotten”), restriction of processing, data portability, and the right to lodge a complaint with an accredited European Data Protection Authority (DPA).

7. Automated Decision-Making and Profiling Disclosures

Ruby Wear Drift does not engage in automated decision-making or algorithmic profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects concerning our patrons. Bespoke fitting allocations, fabric allocations, and tailoring schedules are managed directly by our human concierge staff.

8. Cross-Border Data Transfers and International Protections

As an international couture destination serving clients across North America, Europe, Asia, and the Middle East, personal data may be stored on secure cloud servers located within the United States. When transferring data from the EEA or UK, we implement Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) alongside supplementary encryption safeguards.

9. Data Security Architecture & Retention Protocols

We maintain comprehensive technical, physical, and administrative security measures, including 256-bit TLS encryption, strict role-based access permissions, perimeter firewalls, and regular vulnerability scanning. We retain bespoke anatomical measurement records to facilitate future tailoring commissions for as long as your client account remains active, or until you request deletion.

11. Biometric and Sensitive Personal Information Disclosures

Ruby Wear Drift strictly does not collect, process, or store biometric identifiers, facial recognition scans, genetic data, or political affiliations. Any physical fit measurements or anatomical tailoring adjustments provided during private fitting appointments are utilized strictly with your explicit consent and solely to guarantee bespoke garment construction accuracy and client satisfaction.

12. International Data Transfers and Standard Contractual Safeguards

As an international haute couture atelier welcoming global patrons to our New York salon, personal information submitted through our digital platform may be hosted on secure servers within the United States. When transferring data from the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we implement Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) alongside 256-bit TLS data encryption in transit and at rest.

13. Sensitive Personal Data Governance & Encryption Mandates

Ruby Wear Drift implements rigorous end-to-end cryptographic safeguards (AES-256 and TLS 1.3) across all client measurement databases and private fitting archives. Access to bespoke pattern files and anatomical tailoring notes is restricted strictly to authorized master tailors and senior concierge staff who are bound by lifelong confidentiality and privacy covenants.
