Cookie Policy & Tracking Preferences

Ruby Wear Drift • Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Global Operations Standard

1. Introduction & Purpose of Cookie Architecture

This Cookie Policy explains how Ruby Wear Drift deploys cookies, pixel tags, web beacons, and local storage technologies on rubyweardrift.com. We utilize these digital tools to maintain platform security, preserve user language and fitting preferences, analyze aggregate traffic trends, and protect our salon booking engine from automated spam.

2. Taxonomy of Cookies Deployed

We classify cookies into four distinct functional tiers:

  • Strictly Necessary Cookies: Essential for core website operations, client authentication during fitting salon bookings, security load balancing, and form transmission. These cookies do not store personal identifiers and cannot be disabled.
  • Performance and Analytics Cookies: Collect aggregated, anonymized metrics regarding how visitors interact with our digital journal, page load latency, and clickstream paths (via Google Analytics 4). These insights allow us to optimize our journal typography and interface layout.
  • Functionality and Preference Cookies: Remember your tailored settings—such as measurement unit preferences (inches vs. centimeters), localized currency, or contrast preferences—to deliver an optimized, personalized experience on subsequent visits.
  • Security and Fraud Prevention Cookies: Detect malicious bots, prevent reservation denial-of-service attacks, and verify transactional authenticity across our concierge booking portal.

4. Do Not Track (DNT) and Global Privacy Control (GPC) Signals

Ruby Wear Drift honors universal Global Privacy Control (GPC) and browser-based Do Not Track (DNT) header signals. When our platform detects an active GPC or DNT signal transmitted by your web browser, our digital infrastructure automatically disables all non-essential analytics tracking scripts and third-party measurement pixels without requiring manual configuration.

5. Managing and Revoking Cookie Preferences

You maintain complete autonomy to accept, reject, or customize non-essential cookies. You can manage your preferences through your browser settings or by configuring our on-site cookie banner. Most browsers allow you to view all stored cookies, delete them individually, or block third-party cookies entirely.

7. Cross-Device Tracking and Consent Lifecycle Management

Ruby Wear Drift strictly does not engage in cross-device probabilistic tracking or user fingerprinting across unauthenticated sessions. Your verified cookie preferences remain stored locally on your specific browser terminal for a maximum duration of 12 months, after which our platform will prompt you to review and renew your privacy choices.

8. Automated Preference Sync and Security Verification

Our cookie architecture includes automated preference synchronization across encrypted session tokens. This ensures that your display preferences and measurement units remain preserved across private browsing sessions without transmitting unencrypted personal identifiers over public network channels.

9. Periodic Technological Audits and Regulatory Compliance

Our digital privacy infrastructure undergoes biannual audits by independent cybersecurity professionals to ensure our cookie taxonomy and consent management platforms adhere strictly to updated European EDPB guidelines, California CPRA administrative rules, and emerging global data privacy statutes.

6. Inquiries Regarding Cookie Architecture

For technical inquiries regarding our cookie framework, contact our digital governance office at privacy@rubyweardrift.com or write to Maison Ruby Wear Drift, 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States.
